## Demonstration Website Terms

Northline & Co. is a fictional example used to demonstrate a local-business website. Its people, service categories, prices, address, and testimonials are invented.

### Sample Services and Prices

The service descriptions illustrate where an operating business would explain its offer. They are not an offer to sell a service. No booking or purchase agreement is created by exploring these pages.

### Forms and Confirmation Pages

The contact form demonstrates an inquiry flow. Submit only sample information while testing. Confirmation pages show the next screen in the example flow; they do not confirm a real appointment or purchase.

### External Links

Phone, email, and social destinations are fictional placeholders. Replace them with the actual business destinations before publishing a production site.

### Adapting the Starter

Keep the software license with the project. Replace the example content, business data, and these sample terms with material appropriate to the business that will operate the website.
