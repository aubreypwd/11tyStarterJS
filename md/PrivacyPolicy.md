## About This Demonstration

Northline & Co. is a fictional business. This page is example website content, not a privacy policy for an operating company.

### Information in the Forms

The contact form demonstrates name, email, phone, and project-detail fields. Use fictional details when testing this site. A deployed version may store submissions through its hosting provider when form handling is enabled.

The contact form does not request payment-card information, collect payments, or open accounts.

### Analytics

The starter includes optional analytics support. No tracking script is loaded until a site owner pastes one into the base layout. No client analytics identifier is included in this demo.

### Example Contact Details

The address, phone number, email address, social links, and service areas are placeholders. The reserved northline.example domain does not represent an operating business.

### Before Using This Template

Replace this page with a policy describing the actual operator, data collected, hosting and analytics providers, retention practices, and applicable contact process before launching a real business website.
